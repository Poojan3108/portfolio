VeriSearch

Multi-agent research assistant that checks its own work

Ask any question and four AI agents research it together. A planner breaks it into sub-questions, a researcher searches the web, a writer drafts a cited report, and a critic rejects claims the sources don't support, sending the draft back until it holds up.

4agents in a LangGraph workflow
12web sources per report
3 / 3test runs where the critic forced a revision
10low-reliability domains filtered

ReviewLens

Expert and customer reviews for any product, side by side

Type a product name and ReviewLens finds expert reviews on the web, scores them with a sentiment model I trained, and breaks opinions down by aspect such as battery, comfort, and price. Shoppers can paste Amazon or Best Buy reviews to compare what experts and owners actually say.

HitScope

Two decades of hit music on Spotify and the Billboard Hot 100

An interactive analysis of how hit songs changed from 1999 to 2019, what drives popularity, and whether Spotify hits also chart in the US. It joins Spotify audio features, the Spotify Web API, and Billboard chart history, with K-Means and PCA to find song types.

  • 2025 6th International Conference for Emerging Technology (INCET)
  • 2nd Author
  • Published

Fine-tuned DenseNet121 on 27,000 EuroSAT satellite images across 10 land cover classes, reaching 90.79% accuracy and 91% F1, ahead of EfficientNetB2 and ResNet50.

Semantic Resume Retrieval for Intelligent Recruitment: A Survey and Experimental Evaluation

  • ICACS 2026
  • 2nd Author
  • Accepted · Nov 2026

Built a semantic search pipeline with FAISS and sentence-transformer embeddings over 2,483 resumes in 24 job categories. The smallest model, MiniLM, performed best at 0.877 NDCG@5 while embedding about 8x faster.
